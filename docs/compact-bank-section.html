What the shared-bank test establishes

The extracted circuit contains 22 MOS devices, 16 MIM capacitors, two photodiodes and 293 resistors. It captures both pixels together, deselects and resets them, then reads each retained value twice through the shared output. Dark/dark, dark/bright, bright/dark, medium/medium and bright/bright patterns give the expected brightness order.

Worst output tracking is 281.163 µV. The maximum timestep difference is 0.431 µV; sampled storage placement sensitivity is 0.0375 µV. Both numerical limits are 10 µV. Seven selected placements conserve extracted shunt totals; the raw negative-shunt model remains diagnostic.

Keep the comparisons separate: the physical and bare schematic integrated responses differ by up to 5.821 mV. Changing one pixel's illumination changes its neighbor's output by up to 26.670 µV. These include changes in shared bias, supply and integrated pixel state; they are distinct from same-state capture/readout accuracy.
