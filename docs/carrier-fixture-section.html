ADS1115 and the next measurements

The ADS1115 is a candidate for slow characterization. Its maximum 860 conversions/s means approximately 1.16 ms per conversion, which cannot follow the existing 18 µs column slots. The pixel continues integrating while conversion takes place, so the proposed slow sequence must be validated. See the TI datasheet and the timing assessment. A buffered analog header supports evaluating a faster ADC later.

Next: review schematic ERC, ground returns/decoupling, power sequencing and output-driver stability; validate slow single-pixel acquisition; confirm the spring/spacer stack and wire-bond finish with the chosen providers. The separate unresolved chip clamp-model review still gates full-chip electrical qualification. This checkpoint is not a fabrication release.
