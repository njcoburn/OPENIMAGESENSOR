Die outline, seal-ring interior and usable core are different

The template dimensions give a full die of 3.932 × 5.122 mm, an inside-seal region of 3.880 × 5.070 mm, and a default core of 3.048 × 4.238 mm (12.917 mm²). Dimensions were generated 4 June and checked today. The campaign's 3.88 × 5.07 mm product description refers to the inside-seal region, not the default core.

At 40 µm pitch, 64² pixels reserve 6.554 mm². The actual joined bank uses 2.571 mm². Including the 0.384 mm² row-driver reservation leaves 3.409 mm² of the default core unreserved. This arithmetic checks block bounds and nonoverlap only. The bank already includes one row: a final 4096-pixel assembly must add 63 rows and verify the exact device census. A half-height slot has only 2.479 mm inside the seal in its short dimension, less than the 2.560 mm array; changing the pad ring alone cannot fix that.
