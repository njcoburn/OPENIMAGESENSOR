Resolved: full camera-plus-clamp simulation

The original direct connection now completes all three frames at nominal and hot conditions. The working setup uses abstol=1e-12 (1 pA) and a 1200 s watchdog, retaining reltol=5e-5, gmin=1e-17, chgtol=1e-16, Gear integration, and the original circuit. A 50 ns run of the independent formulation supplies a stricter cross-check, retaining 10 fA absolute tolerance and tightening relative tolerance to 1e-5. Runtime limits prevent runaway jobs; they do not count incomplete runs as passes.

Prior runs at 10 fA slowed sharply around switching events, and the 600 s cutoff stopped them before completion. The validated configuration changes both current tolerance and runtime allowance; these experiments do not establish that either change alone is necessary. This is a diagnosis supported by the controlled comparisons, not a proof of a particular ngspice source-code defect. The ngspice manual defines ABSTOL as the absolute current-error tolerance and documents 1 pA as its default. Accuracy is established here by the measured comparisons, not by that default alone.

Independent check at the original strict tolerance

A second formulation keeps the 10 fA tolerance and introduces an algebraic voltage/current interface in the testbench. Its equations enforce V(CLAMPRAIL)=V(VDD) and return the measured clamp current to VDD. It retains the full nonlinear clamp devices. It is a numerical check; production simulations use the direct connection.

Eclamp CLAMPDRIVE 0 VDD 0 1
Vclamp CLAMPDRIVE CLAMPRAIL 0
Fclamp VDD 0 Vclamp 1
Xsupply CLAMPRAIL 0 sensor_supply_pads

The strict hot refinement also tightens relative tolerance to 1e-5 and monitors voltage, current balance and exchanged power. Transient comparison bounds account for saved waveform precision. Separate finite-source DC/AC diagnostics reveal picoampere-scale numerical differences: the 1 Ω cases meet a 1 pA + 10 ppm current comparison; the 1 kΩ cases exceed that strict screen. Those failures remain recorded. This interface is not used to replace the direct clamp model or to qualify ESD leakage/stress performance.
