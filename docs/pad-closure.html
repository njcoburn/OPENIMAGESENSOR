Candidate power domain

One foundry dvdd plus one dvss pad, with core and IO stripes sharing 3.3 V AVDD/AVSS. This is an electrical candidate; a full ring is not routed. Supply and corner pads contain clamps; adding more changes startup loading. The pinned OCD split-voltage core-supply schematic also uses 6 V clamp devices, so changing libraries alone does not establish thin-oxide protection.

Measured simulation results

  • 324 DC cases: nominal pair leakage 0.146 nA; maximum 76.19 nA across 3.0–3.6 V, −40 to 125°C, and resistor/MOS-capacitor/diode sections. HV MOS remains typical, so this is not full transistor PVT qualification.
  • 24 ideal-source startup cases: 24 settled within 200 µs after the ramp. Fast ramps trigger large modeled clamp current; amp-scale ideal-source peaks are diagnostics, not hardware current ratings.
  • Finite-source tests: 0.5/5 Ω, 100 nF, 33 kΩ load. Original strict tolerances failed in 8/18 cases; those logs are retained. Practical tolerances (absolute current 1 pA, relative 0.01%) failed in 0/18 cases. Of six 1 ms cases, 6 settled. This is a source-impedance sensitivity model, not a selected regulator.
  • Original camera-plus-clamp runs reached the 600 s time limit; original matched controls passed. Separate three-frame tolerance experiments are retained (2/4 practical-current-tolerance screens; 2/4 charge-tolerance screens). The bounded first-frame comparison retains the original electrical and numerical settings: 4/4 pass the existing 0.5 mV sampling, brightness ordering and 1% reference-startup screens. This first-frame result does not establish three-frame clamp repeatability. Both variants use identical compatibility settings, a 1 ms ideal ramp and 200 µs post-ramp reset hold. Simulator configuration and remaining work

The library's multi-finger devices require width-per-finger binning: local ngbehavior=hsa and wnflag=1, following the ngspice manual. No PDK geometry or transistor sizing was changed. Original bin-selection failures are retained.

Use 1 ms soft-start as a candidate, with reset held until the rail is stable. It is not a characterized minimum ramp time. The long coupled camera/clamp transient remains a numerical verification issue. Resolve that before relying on multi-frame results, then check a selected board supply and complete pad-ring connectivity/extraction. Thin-oxide transient voltage, HBM/CDM, pulse heating and assembly qualification still require accepted stress evidence; normal-voltage SPICE and DRC/LVS do not establish ESD protection.
