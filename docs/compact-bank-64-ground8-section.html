64-column ground-bus candidate: physical checks and capture window

The 8 µm ground-bus correction has a separately verified 64-column candidate. Both main DRC checks and both LVS paths pass with 642 MOS devices, 512 MIM plates and 64 photodiodes. A full-layout XOR audit proves that only the intended M4 ground rectangle changes, with unchanged labels and independent reference circuit.

The candidate measures 2629.56 × 978.28 µm, leaving 70.44 µm width and 121.72 µm height inside the 2700 × 1100 µm bank budget. The extra 1.8 µm width does not establish complete-chip fit. This bank already contains one pixel row; final assembly adds 63 rows.

A fresh 200 ns nominal trace completes initialization, capture, row deselection and pixel reset through 1.405 ms in 262.4 wall seconds, with physical MIM plate probes. The original timed-out full trace contains this entire early window; its nine archived file hashes are verified before using that prefix. Every photocurrent source is checked against its corresponding extracted diode, allowing for renamed ground terminals. The remaining stimuli/options match after excluding the model include path, stop time and additional saved probes.

Worst row-deselection STORE movement changes from 2.318 to 2.466 mV. At the revised worst column (62), the mean physical ground plate changes by -2442.608 µV and the across-capacitor voltage by -23.379 µV. These measurements diagnose stored-voltage movement; they are not same-state capture/readout or ADC error measurements and are not judged against the 500 µV output criterion.

The 16-column electrical improvement does not automatically scale. A fresh original-layout capture with identical physical probes reproduces the retained prefix within 0.003237 µV at the six event times. The revised 200→100 ns event comparison differs by at most 0.258784 µV across common saved terminals (10 µV diagnostic comparison limit: pass). The larger revised STORE shift needs further investigation.

Worst actual pre-deselection MIM ground rise drops from 227.588 to 68.647 mV, but remains substantial. A separate resistor-network calculation reduces far-end self-resistance from 129.175 to 63.930 Ω. With an artificial 1 µA test current per column, maximum ground rise falls from 3086.002 to 927.705 µV. Those test currents are not the operating circuit currents; the calculation demonstrates improved extracted resistance, not corrected transient behavior.

Full readout remains open. The old layout reached 14 of 128 readout instants before its 900 s watchdog. A simple extrapolation from its readout phase gives about 99 minutes for the transient alone. The next full experiment should allow a 10,800 s watchdog and budget additional time for 64 capture plus 128 output references. This estimate is not a runtime guarantee; routing and concurrent workloads can change it.

No complete 64-column readout or accuracy pass is claimed. Nominal/hot patterns, timestep and placement checks, process/wire/supply corners, actual local supply/reference drops, real drivers, repeated rows and assembled-chip manufacturing checks remain ahead. Main DRC excludes density, antenna and cup checks.

The subsequent matched far-column readout and distributed-return comparison quantify the output error. Preserve this candidate and the original traces as controls; local bias/supply checks and a complete qualified full-bank scan remain necessary.
